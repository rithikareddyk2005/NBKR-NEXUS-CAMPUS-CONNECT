NBKR Nexus
Enterprise Smart Campus, Student Intelligence & Placement Platform
NBKR Nexus is a comprehensive smart-campus platform designed for "N.B.K.R. Institute of Science & Technology (NBKRIST)".

The platform brings institutional information, student services, academic management, placement intelligence, campus facilities, AI assistance, analytics, and administrative governance into a unified digital ecosystem.

Overview
NBKR Nexus is designed to provide students, faculty, placement officers, and administrators with a centralized platform for managing and accessing important campus services.

The system combines:

🎓 Student Intelligence
📚 Academic Management
💼 Placement Management
🤖 AI Campus Assistant
🏫 Smart Campus Services
📊 Campus Analytics
👨‍🏫 Faculty Services
🛡️ Administration & Governance
🔐 Role-Based Access Control
📡 Future IoT Integration
